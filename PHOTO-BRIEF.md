# Photography brief: Kalamntina website

**Purpose:** replace the placeholder stock photos (hero + four solution cards) with imagery that
looks like Kalamntina's world: senior people, GCC and Jordan settings, decisions being made.
**Owner:** Kalamntina (commission a shoot or license from a regional library).

## Direction

- **People:** Arab and regional professionals, mixed gender, 30–60. Senior, composed, mid-conversation. No posed handshakes, no thumbs-up, no pointing at screens.
- **Settings:** boardrooms, executive offices, training rooms and delivery-centre floors in Amman, Riyadh, Dubai, Doha. Real architecture, not co-working beanbags.
- **Light:** natural window light, soft contrast. Shoot slightly cool; the site desaturates to ~70% and darkens under a navy veil, so avoid strongly warm grading.
- **Composition:** subject on the right third (hero copy sits left on desktop); keep the left 45% calm and low in detail. Leave headroom for crops to 16:9, 4:3 and 3:4.
- **Wardrobe:** business formal and smart national dress (thobe/abaya) where natural. Avoid bright orange or saturated red clothing (clashes with Ignite Orange).
- **Avoid:** laptops as the hero, headphones, startup offices, visible third-party logos, generic Western stock.

Hero and card use **different frames** from the same scene (decision 8A: no photo appears twice).

## Delivery specs

- Minimum 3200px on the long edge, sRGB, JPEG quality 90 or TIFF.
- Model releases for every identifiable person; usage rights: web + social, perpetual.
- File names: `kal-hire-hero.jpg`, `kal-hire-card.jpg`, etc. The site will serve AVIF/WebP at 1x/2x.
